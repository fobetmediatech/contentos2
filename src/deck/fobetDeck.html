09 / PROCESS

How a shoot day
actually runs

This is fixed. Follow it and we finish on time, every time.

D MINUS 7

Topics locked

We send the month's topic list. You approve or swap. No scripting starts before this.

D MINUS 4

Scripts sent

All scripts for the shoot go to you in one document. You have 48 hours to approve or mark changes.

D MINUS 2

Call sheet sent

Location, call time, wardrobe list, props, and which formats we are covering. Avoid stripes, small checks and pure white on camera.

D DAY 10 AM

Setup and first block

Crew arrives 45 minutes early. First block is the easy formats to warm you up.

D DAY 1 PM

Heavy block

Podcast or walkthrough. This is where the energy needs to be highest, so we do it after a break, not at the end.

D DAY 4 PM

B roll and wrap

Cutaways, office shots, hands, product. These make every edit better for the next three months.

D PLUS 5

First edits delivered

Delivered in batches, not all at the end. You get one round of revisions per video.

D PLUS 8

Scheduled and live

Approved edits go into the calendar. Posting starts.

Approvals and

Turnaround times

  • Script approval: 48 hours from your side
  • Edit delivery: 5 working days from shoot
  • Revisions: 1 round per video, within 3 days

If approvals get delayed

Posting continues from the backlog for one week. Beyond that, the calendar shifts and the month's count moves to the following month. We flag it early, not at the end.
